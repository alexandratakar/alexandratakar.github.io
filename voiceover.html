Behind the voice

Finding the feeling
beneath the words.

I’m an aspiring audiobook narrator drawn to character, atmosphere, and the human connection behind a story. My voice is warm and resonant, with a lower register that feels at home in darker worlds.

This portfolio documents the practice: exploring pacing, emotion, and the different ways a story can be heard.
