Development note

Alexandra began as a character I played in a text-based multiplayer roleplaying game (MUD). Her story has since evolved into my own setting, with worldbuilding written by me. Elements tied to the game’s setting and Dungeons & Dragons pantheon have been reworked or replaced as part of that transition.
